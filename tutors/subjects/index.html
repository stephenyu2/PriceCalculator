Your Subjects

Tell us what you can tutor. For each subject you teach, mark your level. Leave the rest blank. This is how we match you to students, so include everything you can cover.

  • Verified: you passed LVT's assessment in this subject.
  • Ready: a subject you could pass our test on today, we just have not tested you yet. You can teach it now with no prep. Checking it means we may place you with a client without testing you first, and delivering is then on you.
  • Backup: not a subject we would assign by default. If we are short we may consider you, usually after a quick check first. An option, not a guarantee. If we ever place you in one of these untested and it does not go well, that is on us, not you.
  • For Learning Differences & Accommodations, these levels mean experience, not a test: Verified = you have tutored students with this and can show it, Ready = comfortable supporting it now, Backup = willing but limited experience.

Mark a level for at least one subject before submitting.

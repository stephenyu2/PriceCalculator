Bank Account and W-9

This is how you get paid. You're paid by direct deposit (ACH) through Relay, our bank. In one short form, Relay collects your bank details and your W-9, so your tax information goes straight to the bank and never touches LVT or this website.

1. Fill out your W-9 now

You'll need a completed W-9 to upload in a moment, and Relay does not give you a blank one. Download it, fill it out, and save it before you go any further.

Download the blank W-9 (IRS)

Upload your W-9 only through the Relay page below. Never email it to us, it has your Social Security number on it.

2. Watch for the Relay email

You'll get an email from Relay titled "Payment info requested from Launch Valley Tutoring LLC." Click Receive Payment and it takes you to a page where you enter your bank details and upload the W-9 you just filled out. Here's what both look like:

When you get paid

We run payroll twice a month, on the 1st and the 15th. The 15th pays for every session in TutorBird dated the 1st through the 15th. The 1st pays for the 16th through the end of the previous month.

TutorBird is the only record we go by. If a session isn't in TutorBird when we run payroll, we have no record of it, and it rolls to the next cycle. Keep your attendance current. Once we initiate the transfer, ACH takes one to two business days to reach your account.

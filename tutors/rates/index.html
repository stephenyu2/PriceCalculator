Tutor Rate Schedule

Launch Valley Tutoring, LLC · Effective July 2026 · All contracted tutors

Every tutor starts at $35 per billable hour and can reach $45 within six months. Billable hours are counted from TutorBird records.

Rates

Tier Title Rate How you get there
1 Associate Tutor $35 Starting rate
2 Tutor $40 25 billable hours or 60 days, whichever comes first
3 Senior Tutor $45 100 billable hours or 6 months, whichever comes first
4 Lead Tutor $50 18 months and 300 billable hours

Hours accumulate from your first session and never reset. Every advancement also requires the Quality Standard below. Rates are never reduced.

Quality Standard

Measured over the trailing 90 days.

  • Zero unexcused no-shows. A missed session without notice is not acceptable at any tier.
  • Client feedback is consistently positive. No unresolved complaint, and families are glad to have you. LVT weighs this as a whole rather than counting incidents.
  • Late cancellations are reviewed case by case. Life happens, and the occasional cancellation inside 24 hours is understood. A pattern of them is not.

Where LVT exercises judgment, a decision to hold a tutor at their current tier is given in writing and names the specific reason.

Reviews

  • Rates are reviewed January 1, April 1, July 1, and October 1.
  • New rates take effect the following pay cycle. They are not retroactive.
  • You may request a review any time you believe you have hit a threshold. You get an answer in writing within 14 days.
  • The number of hours you are assigned is LVT's responsibility, not yours. That is why Tiers 2 and 3 also carry a time-based path.

Rates cover session time. Apart from completing onboarding training and coordinating schedules directly with your families, LVT does not require work outside of sessions. Any preparation or other work you choose to do outside of a session is your own choice and is not paid. What LVT does expect is a strong session, every time. Tutors are independent contractors; this schedule states the rates LVT offers for services and does not guarantee any volume of hours or restrict a tutor from serving other clients.

Your Headshot

Send us a photo for your tutor profile. It goes on the Our Team page of our website, where families can see who you are, so pick one you are happy to have public. This is optional and not part of onboarding.

A clear photo from the shoulders up works best: face fully visible, good lighting, and a plain background if you have one. A phone photo is fine.

JPG or PNG, any size.

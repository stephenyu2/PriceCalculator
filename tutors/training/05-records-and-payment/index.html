Records and Payment Integrity

Why this exists: one TutorBird record determines both what the family is charged and what you are paid.

Enter every session in TutorBird as early as possible. The moment a session is agreed with the parent or with LVT, put it on the calendar. Entering one after the fact is acceptable, but early is the standard. If a family reschedules, update TutorBird right away.

Mark every session within 24 hours.

Which status to pick. TutorBird's attendance statuses, and when to use each:

What happened Mark it as
The session happened Present ($)
The session happened, student arrived late Present, Late ($)
Family cancelled with 24+ hours notice Absent, Notice Given
Family cancelled under 24 hours, or no-showed Absent, No Make-Up Credit ($), and flag it to LVT
You cancelled Tutor Cancelled

Never use either "Issue Make-Up Credit" status ("Absent, Issue Make-Up Credit" or "Tutor Cancelled, Issue Make-Up Credit"), for any reason. Make-up arrangements go through LVT.

Never record a session that did not happen.

Recording a family's cancellation as billable in order to be paid is fraud. It results in immediate termination, recovery of the amount paid, and, depending on the circumstances, a report to the appropriate authorities.

Never accept direct payment from a family, in any form: cash, Venmo, Zelle, gift cards, or anything else. Decline politely and tell LVT the same day.

Never quote a rate, offer a discount, or change a package. Refer the family to LVT.

No side work with LVT families during your time with LVT. If a family asks you to tutor privately, including a sibling, decline and tell LVT they asked. This does not restrict what you do after you leave LVT.

If you forgot to log sessions, log them now and tell LVT they are late. Do not backfill silently.

Records and Payment Integrity

Why this exists: one record in TutorBird decides two separate things. What the family gets charged, and what you get paid. That overlap is exactly where trouble lives.

Mark every session within 24 hours. Not weekly. Not when you remember.

Billable or not:

What happened Billable?
The session happened Yes
Family cancelled with 24+ hours notice No
Family cancelled under 24 hours, or no-showed Mark it and flag it. LVT decides, not you.
You cancelled No
You no-showed No

Never record a session that did not happen. Ever, for any reason, including "we'll make it up next week."

Marking a family's cancellation as billable in order to get paid is fraud. Not a mistake, not a gray area. It results in immediate termination, LVT recovering the money, and, depending on what happened, a report to the appropriate authorities. It is also the easiest thing in this entire business to catch: the parent sees a charge for a session they cancelled, complains within a day, and the complaint points at exactly one person.

Never accept direct payment from a family. No cash, no Venmo, no Zelle, no gift card, no "for the extra twenty minutes," no "just this once." Decline it politely in the moment and tell LVT. This is not about the money. A session LVT does not know about is a session with no record, no insurance, and no one who knows a child is in a room with you.

Never quote a rate, never offer a discount, never change a package. Send them to LVT. You are not authorized to, and getting a rate wrong creates a real problem for a family.

No side work with LVT families while you are with LVT. If a family asks you to tutor a younger sibling privately, the answer is no, and you tell LVT they asked. This is not a restriction on what you do after you leave LVT. It is about what happens while you are here.

If you forgot to log sessions, log them now and tell LVT what happened. Do not estimate silently and do not backfill from memory without saying so.

Session Conduct and Accuracy

Why this exists: students and families rely on what you teach being correct.

If you do not know, say so, out loud: "I'm not certain, let's work it out," or "let me confirm that and get back to you before next session."

Do not guess with confidence in front of a student. An unverified answer gets checked before the student relies on it.

Flag content gaps to Derek. If a topic keeps coming up that you are shaky on, say so.

Do not teach outside the subjects you are qualified for. If a session drifts into one, say so and tell LVT.

Never do the student's work: homework, essays, college applications, take-home tests, or any part of them, regardless of who asks. Decline and tell LVT the same day.

Nothing that violates a school's academic integrity rules, and no help during a live exam of any kind. If you are unsure whether something crosses the line, ask LVT before doing it.

Show up on time and ready. If you must cancel, give as much notice as you can and post in the group chat for coverage; the substitution is final only once LVT confirms it and the family is notified. Unexcused no-shows are disqualifying at every tier of the rate schedule.

Keep your personal views out of sessions: politics, religion, and opinions about a student's school, teachers, or family.

If you taught something wrong, correct it with the student at the next opportunity and tell Derek the same day.

Confidentiality and Student Data

Why this exists: families share information with us that they share with no one else.

What is confidential: student names, schedules, contact information, grades, test scores, diagnoses, IEPs and 504 plans, anything a family tells you about their situation, other tutors' pay rates, your own pay rate, LVT's pricing, LVT's internal processes and materials.

Do not discuss students outside LVT, even without names. Details identify a student to anyone who knows them.

Do not post about students: scores, acceptances, milestones, with or without a name. If something is worth celebrating, tell LVT and we will ask the family.

AI tools: do not put student work, names, scores, essays, IEPs, or any identifying detail into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any other AI tool. If you use AI to build practice material, build it from generic material with nothing identifying in it.

Do not store student data on personal accounts: personal Google Drive, iCloud, Notes, Dropbox, or similar. If you need to store something, ask LVT where.

Lock your devices. Do not leave materials with student names in your car, and do not leave a laptop open at the office.

If you lose something or send it to the wrong person, tell LVT the same day.

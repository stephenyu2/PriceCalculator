Child Safety and Boundaries

Why this exists: tutors work one-on-one with minors. These rules protect students and tutors.

Where sessions happen. In person, sessions happen only inside the Northridge office building: Suite 105, the upstairs conference room, the cafeteria, and the shared areas. Sessions do not happen anywhere else, including coffee shops, parks, libraries, your home, or a student's home. Shared and busier spaces are preferred over private rooms. If a family asks to meet somewhere else, send the request to LVT first.

If the room has a door, it stays open. The whole session, every session. If someone closes it in passing, open it again.

Online sessions run on Zoom, under the same visibility principle.

  • The meeting link goes to the parent or guardian, never to the student directly.
  • The parent may join any session, at any time, without notice. They never need to ask.
  • Your camera stays on for the entire session, and so does the student's.
  • Take the session from a workspace: sitting up at a desk or table, dressed for the office, no bed in frame. Not from a car or a public place such as a coffee shop.
  • No recording, by anyone. If a student starts recording, end the session and tell LVT.
  • No private messaging on the platform. Chat during the session is fine; a direct message before or after it is not.
  • Zoom only. No FaceTime, Discord, Snapchat, personal Google Meet, or a personal Zoom room.

Never transport a student in any vehicle, for any reason. If a student needs transportation, that is the parent's responsibility, or 911's in an emergency.

No private communication with students. Communication involving a student goes through LVT channels or includes a parent: no personal texting, personal email, social media, direct messages of any kind, or gaming platforms. If a student contacts you privately, do not respond on that channel, and tell LVT the same day.

Direct communication with parents is encouraged. You should have the parent's number, and they should have yours. Scheduling, rescheduling, a late pickup, and updates on how sessions are going all go directly between you and the parent.

No physical contact beyond a brief, public gesture such as a handshake. If a student initiates contact, redirect gently and report it the same day.

No gifts, money, rides, loans, or favors between you and a family, in either direction.

No photos, video, or recordings of a student or a student's work, and no posting about a student on any platform, named or not.

No alcohol, cannabis, or any impairing substance before or during a session, or on LVT premises. If in doubt, cancel the session.

No substitutes without approval. If you cannot make a session, post in the group chat for coverage. The substitution is final only once LVT confirms it and the family has been notified.

Report anything that could be misconstrued, including your own conduct, the same day: a student hugs you, a parent asks you to stay late alone, a student tells you something heavy, you were alone in a closed room because someone shut the door. Reporting is never held against you.

If a student becomes attached, keep the boundaries exactly where they are, stay kind and professional inside them, and tell LVT. Do not manage it privately.

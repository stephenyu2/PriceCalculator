Mandated Reporting

Why this exists: whether private tutors are legally mandated reporters in California is unsettled. Every LVT tutor operates as a reporter.

Do not investigate. You do not need to be sure, and you do not need to verify anything. Determining what happened is the authorities' responsibility, not yours.

What you are watching for:

  • Physical: unexplained injuries, injuries that do not match the explanation, injuries in a pattern, a child who flinches
  • Sexual: sexual knowledge or behavior beyond their age, disclosure of any kind
  • Emotional: extreme withdrawal, extreme aggression, a child who describes being terrorized
  • Neglect: consistently hungry, dirty, unsupervised in a way that is not safe for their age, untreated medical needs

What to do:

  • Never promise confidentiality. If a student asks "can you keep a secret," say that you cannot promise that, but you will listen and you will help.
  • Do not interrogate. Do not ask leading questions and do not ask them to repeat it. Let them say what they say.
  • Write it down as soon as the session ends, in their words as closely as you can remember: date, time, what you saw, what they said. Not your conclusions.
  • Tell LVT immediately. Call Stephen, Mark, or Derek.
  • File an incident report the same day.

If you believe a child has been abused or neglected, report it to the authorities regardless of any instruction from anyone at LVT. No one at LVT has the authority to tell you not to report, and there is no retaliation for reporting.

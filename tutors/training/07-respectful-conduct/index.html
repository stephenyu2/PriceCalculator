Respectful Conduct

Why this exists: it is short because the rule is short. It applies in both directions.

Harassment is unwelcome conduct directed at someone because of a protected characteristic: race, color, national origin, religion, sex, gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, age, disability, medical condition, marital status, veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by law. It includes slurs, jokes, comments about someone's body, unwanted advances, and repeated conduct that a reasonable person would find hostile.

Abusive conduct is separate and also prohibited: repeated hostility, intimidation, humiliation, or sabotage of someone's work.

This applies to how you treat students, families, other tutors, and LVT staff. All of it. Zero tolerance.

It also applies to how you get treated. If a parent, a student, another tutor, or anyone at LVT harasses you, report it to Derek at (818) 441-2204 or launch@launchvalleytutoring.com. If your complaint is about Derek, report it to Stephen at (818) 294-3292 instead. You do not have to handle it yourself, you do not have to be sure it "counts," and you do not have to be polite about it first.

No retaliation. Nobody's hours get cut, nobody's tier gets held, nobody gets fewer families because they reported something. If you think that is happening, say so immediately.

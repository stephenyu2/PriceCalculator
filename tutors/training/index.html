Compliance Training

Seven modules. Read each one in full, answer the questions at the end, and mark it complete. When all seven are done, submit one completion form. Budget about thirty minutes, and try to do it in one sitting.

Module 1: Child Safety and Boundaries

The rules that keep every session visible, and that protect students and tutors alike.

Module 2: Mandated Reporting

What to watch for and what to do. Every LVT tutor operates as a reporter.

Module 3: Emergencies and Incidents

What to do in the moment, and the same-day written record that follows.

Module 4: Session Conduct and Accuracy

Saying "I don't know" out loud, never doing the student's work, and showing up ready.

Module 5: Records and Payment Integrity

One TutorBird record decides what a family is charged and what you are paid.

Module 6: Confidentiality and Student Data

Families tell us things they tell no one else. That trust is the product.

Module 7: Respectful Conduct

A short rule, and it applies in both directions.

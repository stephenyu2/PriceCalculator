Independent Contractor Tutor Agreement

Launch Valley Tutoring LLC

The requirements in this Agreement are safety, legal, and record-keeping requirements. They exist because our students are minors and because our attendance records determine what families are charged and what you are paid. How you teach is up to you.

I seek to enter into an independent contractor relationship with Launch Valley Tutoring LLC (the "Company") voluntarily to provide tutoring services to the Company as more fully described in this Agreement. Neither I nor the Company has agreed to maintain this relationship for any specified period of time. Accordingly, either the Company or I may terminate the relationship at any time, with or without cause, for any lawful reason, subject to the terms of this Agreement.

Independent Contractor

I understand that I am intended to be engaged as an independent contractor to, and not as an employee of, the Company. I acknowledge that I have sole responsibility for payment of all federal, state, and local taxes and other amounts due on payments received by me from the Company for my services, except to the extent otherwise required by applicable law. I further understand and agree that I am not eligible for, and expressly disclaim any interest in, compensation plans and benefits offered to employees of the Company, including but not limited to bonus plans, vacation or paid time off, retirement plans, life insurance, medical insurance, disability insurance, workers' compensation insurance, unemployment benefits, or any other employee benefit.

I understand that, except as may be necessary to coordinate sessions and meet client needs, the Company does not control the manner and means by which I perform tutoring services. I am responsible for determining the methods, materials, and approach used in performing services, subject to client expectations and the standards set forth in this Agreement. Except as otherwise agreed for scheduling purposes, I remain responsible for my own equipment, supplies, transportation, licenses, and work-related expenses. I understand that I am free to engage in other employment, occupation, consulting, or business activity, provided that I do not use or disclose the Company's confidential information or interfere with my obligations under this Agreement.

Compensation

I understand that my compensation will be communicated to me separately by the Company in writing, including by email or other written notice. The Company may, from time to time during the term of this Agreement, propose modifications to compensation on a prospective basis only. If I do not agree to any such modification, I will notify the Company promptly, and either party may elect to end the relationship going forward. If I continue to provide services after receiving notice of the modification and do not object in writing, the modified compensation terms will apply prospectively.

I understand that the Company will pay me on a monthly basis, based on the Company's own records and tracking of tutoring sessions and hours actually completed. Payment will generally be made at or around the end of each month, or within a reasonable time thereafter, provided the services reflected in the Company's records are accurate and verifiable.

I also understand that:

  • I will be compensated only for tutoring services actually rendered and any other time expressly approved in writing by the Company;
  • I will not be compensated for unscheduled waiting time or time not actually spent providing approved services;
  • The Company does not guarantee me any minimum amount of work, assignments, or compensation.

Modification of Terms and Conditions

The Company reserves the right, in its discretion and to the extent permitted by law, to modify this Agreement prospectively. I understand and agree that I may be required to electronically sign any updated agreement before continuing to provide services to the Company.

Restrictions and Obligations

I agree:

  • to comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations in providing services;
  • to perform services in a professional, competent, and ethical manner;
  • not to make false, misleading, or unauthorized statements on behalf of the Company;
  • not to disclose or misuse confidential information belonging to the Company, its clients, students, families, or tutors.

Child Safety and Conduct

I understand that the Company's clients include minors, and that the requirements in this section are material terms of this Agreement. I agree that:

  • I will conduct in-person sessions only inside the Company's Northridge office building, and at no other physical location. If I am in a room with a door, that door will stay open for the entire session. I will not conduct a session behind a closed door.
  • I will conduct online sessions only on the platform the Company designates. The session link goes to the parent or guardian and never to the student directly. The parent or guardian may join any session at any time without notice. My camera will stay on for the entire session. I will not record a session, and I will not conduct one from a bed or a vehicle.
  • I will not meet with a student outside of a scheduled session recorded in the Company's systems.
  • I will not transport a student in any vehicle, for any reason.
  • I will not have physical contact with a student beyond a brief, ordinary, public gesture such as a handshake.
  • I will not communicate privately with a student. All communication with a student or family will go through Company channels or will include a parent or guardian. I will not use personal texting, personal email, direct messages, social media, gaming platforms, or any other private channel to contact a student.
  • I will not give or receive gifts, money, favors, rides, or anything of value to or from a student or family.
  • I will not photograph, film, or record a student, and I will not post about a student on any platform, including anonymously.
  • I will not be under the influence of alcohol, cannabis, or any impairing substance during a session, before a session, or while on Company premises.
  • I will not permit any other person to conduct a session in my place unless the Company has confirmed the substitution in advance and the family has been notified.
  • I will report to the Company immediately any conduct by any person, including my own, that could reasonably raise a child safety concern or be misconstrued as one.

I understand that violating any requirement in this section is grounds for immediate termination of this Agreement.

Required Training

I understand that completing the Company's required onboarding and compliance training is a condition of providing services, that I must complete it before my first session, and that the Company may require periodic re-acknowledgment of updated materials. The Company will separately notify me of any compensation for time spent completing required training.

Company Policies

I agree to comply with the Company's written policies as provided to me and as updated from time to time, including its child safety, incident reporting, confidentiality, and records policies. Those policies are incorporated into this Agreement by reference. The Company will give notice of material changes, and I understand I may be required to acknowledge updated policies electronically before continuing to provide services.

Accuracy of Session Records

I understand that the Company's records of session attendance determine both what a family is charged and what I am paid, and that I am responsible for recording my sessions accurately and promptly. I agree that:

  • I will record each session's attendance and billable status accurately, and I will not record a session that did not occur.
  • I will not record a session as billable when I cancelled it, or when the family cancelled with the notice required under the Company's client agreement.
  • I understand that knowingly recording a session inaccurately in order to obtain payment is grounds for immediate termination, that the Company may recover any amount paid in error, and that it may be reported to the appropriate authorities.

No Direct Payment

I will not accept payment in any form, directly from a student or family, for any tutoring or related service. All payment for tutoring flows through the Company. If a family offers me direct payment, I will decline it and inform the Company.

No Authority to Bind

I have no authority to enter into agreements, set or change rates, offer discounts, make commitments, or otherwise bind the Company, and I will not represent that I do.

Company Clients During the Term

During the term of this Agreement, I will not provide tutoring or related instructional services to any Company client, or to any student of a Company client, outside of the Company. This restriction applies only while this Agreement is in effect. Nothing in this section restricts my ability to work for other clients or businesses, and nothing in this section restricts my activities after this Agreement ends, except as provided in the Confidential Information section and as permitted by California law.

Background Check

I understand that my engagement is conditioned on completion of a background check to the Company's satisfaction, and that the Company may conduct additional checks during the term. I will complete the separate disclosure and authorization required for that check.

Ongoing Disclosure

I represent that I am not required to register as a sex offender in any jurisdiction, and that I have never been terminated, suspended, or asked to resign from any position involving minors for reasons relating to the safety or treatment of a minor. If either of these changes during the term, or if I am arrested for or charged with any offense involving a minor or any violent or sexual offense, I will notify the Company within 72 hours.

Cooperation and Preservation

If the Company receives a complaint, claim, inquiry, or legal process relating to my services, I will cooperate reasonably with the Company's response, including providing information and preserving all records, messages, notes, and materials in my possession relating to the matter. I will not delete or alter any such record. This obligation continues after this Agreement ends.

Indemnification

I will indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Company and its members, officers, and agents from and against any claim, loss, liability, damage, or expense, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of (a) my breach of this Agreement, (b) my gross negligence or willful misconduct, or (c) my violation of law. This obligation does not apply to the extent a claim arises from the Company's own negligence or willful misconduct. This obligation continues after this Agreement ends.

Insurance

I am responsible for my own insurance. The Company does not provide me with liability, health, disability, or workers' compensation coverage. If the Company requests, I will obtain and maintain general liability insurance in an amount the Company reasonably specifies and provide proof of coverage.

Notice and Electronic Records

I consent to receive notices, agreements, policies, and other records from the Company electronically, including by email to the address I provide and by posting to the Company's tutor portal. I agree that my electronic acknowledgment of any such record has the same legal effect as a handwritten signature. I will keep my contact information current with the Company.

Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, that provision will be limited or severed to the minimum extent necessary, and the remaining provisions will remain in full force and effect.

Survival

The sections titled Confidential Information, Return of Company Property, Injunctive Relief, Cooperation and Preservation, Indemnification, Severability, Choice of Law and Venue, and this section survive termination of this Agreement.

Confidential Information

During the course of my relationship with the Company, I may receive or have access to confidential or proprietary information, including but not limited to client identities, student records, academic information, schedules, contact information, pricing, tutor pay rates, internal processes, business plans, marketing information, and other non-public information (collectively, "Confidential Information"). I agree not to use Confidential Information except as necessary to perform services for the Company and not to disclose it to any third party except as authorized in writing by the Company or as required by law.

The obligations in this section do not apply to information that: (a) becomes publicly available through no fault of mine; (b) was lawfully known to me prior to disclosure by the Company; or (c) is lawfully obtained by me from a third party without breach of any duty.

Non-Solicitation of Clients During Relationship

During the term of this Agreement, I will not directly solicit for competing services any client of the Company for whom I performed services through the Company or whose identity I learned solely through my relationship with the Company, if such solicitation uses the Company's Confidential Information or is otherwise in breach of this Agreement. Nothing in this Agreement is intended to prohibit lawful competition after the relationship ends except to the extent permitted by applicable California law with respect to confidentiality, trade secrets, and non-solicitation based on misuse of confidential information.

Return of Company Property

Upon request by the Company or upon termination of this Agreement, I will promptly return or permanently delete, as applicable, all Company property and Confidential Information in my possession or control, including physical documents, electronic files, access credentials, and materials containing client or student information, except to the extent retention is required by law.

Injunctive Relief

I agree that any actual or threatened misuse of Confidential Information or other material breach of the confidentiality provisions of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm to the Company for which monetary damages may be inadequate. Accordingly, the Company may seek injunctive or other equitable relief to prevent or remedy such breach, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

Warranties

I represent and warrant that: (a) I will perform the services in a professional and lawful manner; (b) I have the experience, qualifications, and ability necessary to provide the services I agree to perform; and (c) my entering into this Agreement and performing services under it will not violate any agreement or duty I owe to any third party.

Accurate Representation

I affirm that I have provided accurate, current, and complete information to the Company regarding my qualifications, experience, background, and other information relevant to the services I provide, and I agree to update such information as necessary during the term of this Agreement.

No Assignment

I may not assign or delegate my rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the Company.

Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Company and me concerning the subject matter described herein and supersedes any prior oral or written understandings on that subject, except for any separate written compensation notice, invoice procedure, or service-specific policy expressly incorporated by reference.

Choice of Law and Venue

This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or relating to it will be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California, without regard to its conflict of laws rules. Any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement will be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Los Angeles County, California, and I consent to the jurisdiction of those courts.

Electronic Signature

Signing this document may be completed electronically. By digitally signing this Agreement, I affirm that I have carefully read and understand this Agreement, agree to be bound by its terms and conditions, and intend for my electronic signature to be treated as the legal equivalent of my handwritten signature.

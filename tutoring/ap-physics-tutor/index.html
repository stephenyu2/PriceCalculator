What AP Physics Tutoring Covers

AP Physics comes in three versions — Physics 1, Physics 2, and Physics C — and each one has its own set of demands. Physics 1 covers mechanics, waves, and simple circuits. Physics 2 moves into fluids, thermodynamics, optics, and more. Physics C brings in calculus-based mechanics and electromagnetism. Whichever course your student is in, the AP exam will ask them to set up problems from scratch, pick the right principles, and justify their answers in writing.

AP Physics 1 Unit Breakdown

Unit Key Topics % of Exam
Unit 1 – Kinematics Position, velocity, acceleration, motion graphs, projectile motion 12–18%
Unit 2 – Forces & Newton's Laws Free-body diagrams, Newton's 1st, 2nd & 3rd laws, friction, tension 16–20%
Unit 3 – Circular Motion & Gravitation Centripetal acceleration, uniform circular motion, Newton's law of gravitation 6–8%
Unit 4 – Energy Work, kinetic & potential energy, conservation of energy, power 20–28%
Unit 5 – Momentum Impulse, conservation of momentum, elastic & inelastic collisions 12–18%
Unit 6 – Simple Harmonic Motion Springs, pendulums, period and frequency, energy in oscillations 4–6%
Unit 7 – Torque & Rotational Motion Torque, rotational kinematics, moment of inertia, angular momentum 12–18%
Unit 8 – Electric Charge & Force Coulomb's law, electric field, charge distributions 4–6%
Unit 9 – DC Circuits Current, resistance, Ohm's law, series & parallel circuits, power 16–20%
Unit 10 – Mechanical Waves & Sound Wave properties, interference, standing waves, the Doppler effect 12–16%

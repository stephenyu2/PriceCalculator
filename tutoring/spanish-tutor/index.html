Spanish Tutoring in Northridge

A lot of students in Northridge do fine in Spanish 1 and Spanish 2. Then junior year hits. Suddenly there's the subjunctive, AP Spanish essays, and oral exams — and what worked before stops working. If your student is putting in the time but still struggling, a Spanish tutor Northridge families trust can make a real difference.

What We Cover in Spanish Tutoring

Our tutors work on the areas that actually trip students up. Every session is built around what the student needs right now — not a one-size-fits-all lesson plan.

  • Core grammar: ser vs. estar, preterite vs. imperfect, subjunctive mood
  • Reading comprehension and written assignments
  • Conversation practice and oral exam preparation
  • AP Spanish Language and Culture: email reply, argumentative essay, and spoken tasks

Launch Valley Tutoring is based in Northridge and serves students throughout the San Fernando Valley.

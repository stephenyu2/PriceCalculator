What AP English Language Tutoring Covers

The AP English Language exam has three free-response essays: rhetorical analysis, argument, and synthesis. Each one requires a different approach. Rhetorical analysis asks students to explain how an author builds their case — the choices they made and why those choices work. The argument essay asks students to take a clear position and defend it with specific evidence. The synthesis essay asks students to pull ideas from multiple sources and weave them into a focused response.

At Launch Valley Tutoring, sessions go beyond reviewing vocabulary lists or grammar rules. Tutors work through real College Board prompts with students, giving direct feedback on thesis strength, evidence selection, and commentary. Students learn what a high-scoring response actually looks like — and how to write one themselves, not just recognize one.

Rhetorical Analysis Analyze how an author uses rhetorical choices (appeals, diction, structure, tone) to advance their argument ~40 minutes
Argument Develop and defend a well-reasoned position on a given topic using evidence, reasoning, and commentary ~40 minutes

How Intensive Review Sessions Work

Our tutors don't waste time. In the first session, they figure out exactly where your student stands. What do they know well? Where are the gaps? From there, every minute is spent on the material that's most likely to show up on the exam. We skip what they already get and dig into what they don't. Sessions include practice problems so students aren't just reading — they're actually doing the work and building real confidence before exam day.

At Launch Valley Tutoring in Northridge, we've helped students across the San Fernando Valley go from anxious to ready in just a few sessions. One-on-one review moves fast because it's built around that specific student, not a whole class.

Finals Prep Timeline

Weeks Before Finals What to Focus On Action Items
4 Weeks Out Big-picture planning
  • List every exam and its date
  • Identify the heaviest subjects
  • Book your first tutor session
3 Weeks Out Concept review
  • Re-read key chapters and notes
  • Flag topics you don't understand
  • Start making summary sheets per subject
2 Weeks Out Active practice
  • Work through past tests and problem sets
  • Focus tutor sessions on weak areas
  • Quiz yourself without looking at notes
1 Week Out Targeted drilling
  • Redo every problem you got wrong
  • Prioritize the highest-weight topics
  • Limit new material — reinforce what you know
Night Before Light review and rest
  • Scan your summary sheets only
  • No all-nighters — sleep improves recall
  • Lay out materials and set your alarm

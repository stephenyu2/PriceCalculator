What AP Psychology Tutoring Covers

The AP Psychology exam tests students across nine main content areas: biological bases of behavior, sensation and perception, states of consciousness, learning, cognition, developmental psychology, motivation and emotion, social psychology, and psychological disorders and treatment. That's a lot of ground to cover.

At Launch Valley Tutoring in Northridge, tutors don't just help students memorize terms — they help students understand them. Sessions focus on connecting vocabulary to real examples, learning the landmark studies that show up on the exam, and practicing the free-response questions that require students to apply concepts accurately. Most students struggle most with the FRQs. We spend real time on those.

Who Benefits from AP Psychology Tutoring

This is a good fit for students who feel behind on the material, students who read their notes but still can't quite explain the concepts, and students who want to push their score from a 3 to a 4 or 5. It also helps students who took the class for college credit and want to actually be prepared when exam day arrives.

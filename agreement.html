1. Services

Launch Valley Tutoring ("LVT," "we," "us") provides academic tutoring and test-preparation services. Tutoring is delivered by qualified tutors engaged as independent contractors. The parent or guardian ("you") is responsible for the account and all payments.

2. Plans and Payment

We offer two ways to pay. Flexible Hour Packages are prepaid blocks of tutoring hours (such as 10 or 20 hours) purchased at the rate for your student's level, and purchased hours never expire. The Monthly Subscription is a recurring plan that reserves a weekly time slot for your student at a set number of hours per week; it is billed automatically each month on the same date to the payment method on file and renews until canceled. All payments are processed securely through Stripe. Rates are set by grade level and provided at the time of purchase.

3. Refunds

All payments are final and non-refundable. Prepaid package hours are non-refundable, though they never expire and remain available for future sessions. Monthly subscription payments already processed are non-refundable; canceling a subscription stops future billing but does not refund the current or prior months.

4. Scheduling

For the Monthly Subscription, your student's weekly time slot is reserved and held, generally with the same tutor each week; if your regular tutor is unavailable, we will cover the session with another qualified tutor. Any additional sessions outside your reserved slot, including redeeming make-up credits, are booked the same way as Flexible Hour Package sessions (described next). For Flexible Hour Packages, sessions are booked as needed with no reserved slot; booking at least 48 hours in advance guarantees a session with a subject-expert tutor, though a specific tutor or exact time is not guaranteed, and booking earlier improves your chances of your preferred time and tutor. Sessions may be booked up to two weeks in advance.

5. Cancellations, Rescheduling, and Missed Sessions

Cancel or reschedule with at least 24 hours' notice at no penalty. For the Monthly Subscription, a session canceled with at least 24 hours' notice is still billed as part of your monthly plan, and we will issue a make-up credit for one session of your student's usual length; make-up credits appear in your TutorBird account, are your responsibility to redeem, and expire 30 days after they are issued. Make-up credits are booked like any non-reserved session (see Scheduling above): the earlier you book the better, and no more than two weeks in advance. For Flexible Hour Packages, a session canceled with at least 24 hours' notice is simply not deducted from your hour balance (and your hours never expire). Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice, and no-shows, are billed or deducted as a used session and receive no make-up credit. Repeated no-call, no-show sessions may incur a fee.

6. Subscription Pauses

If your student will be away for an extended period (two weeks or more, such as a vacation), you may pause your subscription. Billing stops during the pause and resumes when you return, and we will do our best to restore your original time slot.

7. Canceling a Subscription

You may cancel your monthly subscription at any time. Cancellation stops future billing, and you will not be charged for the following month.

8. What's Included (Monthly Subscription)

Subscriptions include a reserved weekly time slot, full access to our curriculum, homework between sessions on request, progress quizzes on request, and a monthly parent-tutor conference on request.

9. Results

We are committed to your student's progress, but we do not guarantee specific grades, test scores, or academic outcomes. Results depend on many factors, including the student's effort, attendance, and engagement.

10. Conduct

We ask that students and families treat our tutors with respect. We reserve the right to decline or discontinue service in cases of abusive conduct or repeated policy violations.

11. Changes to These Terms

We may update these terms from time to time. Material changes to subscription rates will be communicated with at least 30 days' notice.

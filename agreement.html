Tutoring Services Agreement

Please read these terms carefully. By checking the boxes and submitting your name and email below, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to these terms.

1. Services

Launch Valley Tutoring ("LVT," "we," "us") provides academic tutoring and test-preparation services. Tutoring is delivered by qualified tutors engaged as independent contractors. The parent or guardian ("you") is responsible for the account and all payments.

2. How It Works: Prepaid Hours

All tutoring is purchased as prepaid hours. You purchase a package of hours, and each completed session is deducted from your hour balance. Your balance is visible in your TutorBird account. Purchased hours never expire. Hours may be used for any of our services, but hours are unique to the enrolled student and cannot be shared or transferred, including among siblings. Rates are set by your student's level and by the size of the package purchased; larger packages are offered at a lower per-hour rate. Families with two or more enrolled students receive a 10% sibling discount on refill purchases. Current rates are available in your parent portal.

3. Introductory Package

New families begin with a 6-hour introductory package at 20% off, backed by our money-back guarantee. If you are not satisfied, you may request a full refund of the introductory package, regardless of how many of the six hours were used, at any time before you purchase additional hours or within 14 days of your sixth introductory session, whichever comes first. After that point, the introductory payment is final. This guarantee applies only to the introductory package; hours purchased afterward are governed by Section 7.

4. Saved Payment Method and Automatic Refill

When you purchase your introductory package, your payment method is securely saved with our payment processor, Stripe. You authorize LVT to charge that saved payment method for tutoring hours you purchase, including when you are not present at checkout.

No automatic charge will occur unless you enroll in Automatic Refill. If you choose to continue with us after your introductory package, you may enroll by selecting a refill size of 5, 10, or 20 hours. Once enrolled, we will charge your saved payment method for your selected refill size whenever your hour balance falls to two hours or below, at the per-hour rate for your student's level and package size. Refills are not charged on a fixed date; they occur only when your balance reaches this threshold, so their frequency depends on how quickly you use your hours.

We will email you before each automatic refill is charged. You may change your refill size, pause automatic refill, or turn it off entirely at any time by contacting us, and requests take effect within one business day. Turning off automatic refill does not affect hours you have already purchased. We require a valid payment method on file and strongly recommend a backup card. If a charge fails on all cards on file, we will notify you, and no further sessions may be scheduled until payment is resolved.

5. Scheduling

Once your student is matched with a tutor, you coordinate scheduling directly with that tutor. Sessions are booked from your available hour balance. When your balance reaches zero, no further sessions may be scheduled until your account is refilled. We do not reserve or guarantee a fixed weekly time slot; recurring times are arranged between you and your tutor and are subject to that tutor's availability.

6. Cancellations, Rescheduling, and Missed Sessions

Cancel or reschedule with at least 24 hours' notice and no hours are deducted from your balance. For cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice, and for no-shows, we reserve the right to deduct the session from your hour balance as a used session. If a tutor cancels a session, no hours are deducted.

7. Unused Hours and Account Closure

Purchased hours never expire and remain available for future sessions, so we do not issue refunds simply because hours are unused. If you wish to close your account and cash out your remaining balance, we will refund your unused hours at 75% of their purchase value, returned to your original payment method. To request this, contact us directly; for your security, we confirm all account and refund requests with the account holder before processing them. The introductory package is governed by Section 3.

8. Included Benefits

Every enrollment includes the following at no additional charge: homework between sessions, assigned by your tutor on request; weekly tests on request, with optional proctoring at our Northridge office (academic students receive a standard diagnostic test, and SAT/ACT prep students receive a full-length practice exam); study access to our office for enrolled students; a monthly parent-tutor conference, on request; a one-time, one-hour meeting with a test-taking expert, on request; and our tutor rematch guarantee: if your tutor is not the right fit, we will match your student with a new tutor at no charge, and the first session with the new tutor is free. These benefits are complimentary, have no cash value, are not refundable or exchangeable for tutoring hours, and are subject to scheduling and availability. We may reasonably adjust how these benefits are delivered.

9. Results

We are committed to your student's progress, but we do not guarantee specific grades, test scores, or academic outcomes. Results depend on many factors, including the student's effort, attendance, and engagement.

10. Conduct

We ask that students and families treat our tutors with respect. We reserve the right to decline or discontinue service in cases of abusive conduct or repeated policy violations.

11. Changes to These Terms

We may update these terms from time to time. Material changes to rates or to the automatic refill terms will be communicated with at least 30 days' notice.

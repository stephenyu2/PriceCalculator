Cancel & Refund

Closing your account cashes out your unused hours at 75% of their purchase value, refunded to your original payment method (per Section 7 of the Tutoring Services Agreement). For your security, we confirm every cancellation and refund with the account holder directly before processing, so this one isn't a form. Copy the message below and send it to us.

Hi, I'd like to close my account and cash out my remaining hour balance. I understand unused hours are refunded at 75% of their purchase value to my original payment method, per the Tutoring Services Agreement.

Send it to your point of contact, whether that's Stephen, Mark, or Derek, or email it to launch@launchvalleytutoring.com. We'll confirm with you directly and process the refund.

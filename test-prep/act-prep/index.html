ACT Prep

The ACT is a curriculum-based test that covers English, Mathematics, Reading, and Science across four distinct sections — all in a single sitting that runs close to three hours with the optional essay. Unlike the SAT, the ACT is not adaptive, but its sheer pace makes it one of the most demanding standardized tests a high school student will face. The English section moves through 75 grammar and rhetoric questions in just 45 minutes, the Math section includes trigonometry and covers topics that go slightly beyond SAT Math, the Reading section requires rapid passage analysis with almost no time to reread, and the Science section demands fluent interpretation of charts, graphs, and conflicting scientific viewpoints — even if the student hasn't taken advanced science courses yet.

At Launch Valley Tutoring, our ACT prep tutors build a customized plan for each student based on a full diagnostic assessment across all four sections. We identify which content areas and question types are costing the most points, then structure sessions around targeted practice and strategy-building rather than generic review. Our tutors are well-versed in the specific patterns the ACT uses repeatedly — the kinds of grammar errors English tests over and over, the chart-reading shortcuts that make Science manageable, the question order strategies that help students finish Reading without panicking. Because every session is one-on-one, tutors can adjust on the fly and make sure students are genuinely internalizing the material.

Students who commit to ACT prep with Launch Valley Tutoring regularly see composite score increases of 3 to 6 points or more, which can make the difference between a good application and a great one.

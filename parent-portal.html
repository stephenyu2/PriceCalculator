Scheduling & Cancellations

You and your tutor set your schedule directly, week to week.

  • 24+ hours' notice: reschedule or cancel with your tutor and no hours are deducted.
  • Under 24 hours or a no-show: the session may be deducted from your balance.
  • If your tutor cancels: no hours are ever deducted.

Discounts

Two ways to save on your recurring plan.

Sibling Discount

Have two or more students enrolled? Your family gets 10% off every refill, for all siblings, automatically. Just let us know both students are enrolled when you set up your plan.

Referral Discount

Refer another family and you both get $50 off your next refill. The credit applies to the first package the family you referred buys after their intro package, not to the intro package itself.

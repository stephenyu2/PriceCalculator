Scheduling & Cancellations

You and your tutor set your schedule directly, week to week.

  • 24+ hours' notice: reschedule or cancel with your tutor and no hours are deducted.
  • Under 24 hours or a no-show: the session may be deducted from your balance.
  • If your tutor cancels: no hours are ever deducted.

What Every Enrollment Includes

Every enrolled family gets these six perks at no extra cost. For anything marked "on request," just ask your tutor or contact us below.

  • ✓

    Homework between sessions, assigned by your tutor on request, so progress doesn't stop when the session ends.

  • ✓

    Weekly tests on request: a standard diagnostic for academic students, a full-length SAT/ACT practice exam for test prep. Optional proctoring at our Northridge office, Sundays at 10 AM.

  • ✓

    Office access. Our Northridge office is open to enrolled students during normal business hours. Need a quiet place to study? Drop in, no booking needed.

  • ✓

    Monthly parent-tutor conference, on request. Sit down with your student's tutor each month to review progress and goals.

  • ✓

    A one-hour meeting with a test-taking expert, on request. A one-time session covering strategy, pacing, and test-day nerves.

  • ✓

    Tutor rematch guarantee. If your tutor isn't the right fit, we'll rematch you free, and the first session with your new tutor is on us.

Math Tutoring in Northridge

Math

From Pre-Algebra to AP Calculus, our math tutors help students build confidence and master every concept. Whether your student is struggling with the basics or pushing for a 5 on the AP exam, we match them with a tutor who meets them exactly where they are.

Pre-Algebra+

Pre-Algebra builds the foundation every future math course depends on - variables, ratios, basic geometry, and early functions. Students who leave gaps here carry them into Algebra 1 and beyond. Our tutors close those gaps systematically so students enter their next course ready. Learn about Pre-Algebra tutoring →

Algebra 1+

Algebra 1 is the gateway to all higher math and the course where many students first hit a wall. Our tutors break down linear equations, inequalities, systems, and graphing into clear steps, staying in sync with your student's class pacing so help is always timely and relevant. Learn about Algebra 1 tutoring →

Algebra 2+

Algebra 2 moves fast through functions, logarithms, polynomials, and complex numbers. Our tutors connect new material to what students already know from Algebra 1, keeping grades from slipping and making sure concepts become second nature before moving on. Learn about Algebra 2 tutoring →

Geometry+

Geometry demands proof-based reasoning and spatial thinking - a style of math many students haven't encountered before. Our tutors build both the conceptual understanding and the step-by-step proof-writing skills that carry students through the year. Learn about Geometry tutoring →

Pre-Calculus+

Pre-Calculus is the final preparation before Calculus, covering trigonometry, conic sections, and function analysis at a demanding level. Students who enter Calculus without a solid Pre-Calculus foundation typically struggle from week one. Our tutors make sure that doesn't happen. Learn about Pre-Calculus tutoring →

Trigonometry+

Trigonometry is concept-dense and notation-heavy - the unit circle, identities, inverse functions, and graphing all need to be fluent before students can move forward. Our tutors build the pattern recognition that makes trig problems manageable rather than mysterious. Learn about Trigonometry tutoring →

AP Calculus AB+

AP Calculus AB covers limits, derivatives, and integrals at a college level. The free-response section requires precise multi-step reasoning under time pressure. Our tutors are well-versed in the specific question types the College Board tests and help students build the fluency to handle them confidently. Learn about AP Calculus AB tutoring →

AP Calculus BC+

AP Calculus BC covers everything in AB plus sequences, series, parametric equations, and polar coordinates - the most advanced math course available to most high school students. Our tutors help students keep pace with the demanding curriculum while building exam-ready mastery. Learn about AP Calculus BC tutoring →

AP Statistics+

AP Statistics covers data analysis, probability, and inference, and many students underestimate it until the first exam. The AP exam is writing-heavy, requiring students to explain their reasoning rather than just calculate. Our tutors teach both the content and the communication skills the exam rewards. Learn about AP Statistics tutoring →

Integrated Math+

Integrated Math 1, 2, and 3 blend algebra, geometry, and statistics into a single curriculum. The approach works for some students and creates real confusion for others. Our tutors are experienced with the integrated sequence and help students build a coherent understanding across all three courses. Learn about Integrated Math tutoring →

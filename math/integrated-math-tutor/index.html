Integrated Math vs. Traditional Math: What's the Difference?

Most parents grew up with separate courses — Algebra 1, then Geometry, then Algebra 2. California's Integrated Math sequence blends those subjects across three years. Here's how the courses line up:

Course IM Equivalent Key Topics Covered
IM 1 Algebra 1 + Geometry intro Linear equations, systems of equations, basic geometry, introductory statistics
IM 2 Geometry + Algebra 2 intro Quadratics, geometric transformations, right triangle trigonometry, probability
IM 3 Algebra 2 + Pre-Calc intro Exponential and logarithmic functions, polynomial operations, advanced statistics

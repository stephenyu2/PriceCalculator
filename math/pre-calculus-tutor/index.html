Pre-Calculus Tutoring in Northridge

A lot of students coast through Algebra 2 and think Pre-Calculus will be more of the same. Then week three hits and suddenly there are six trig identities to memorize, conic sections that look nothing like anything they've seen before, and a teacher moving faster than ever. Pre-Calculus is the last chance to fill in any math gaps before Calculus, and many students don't realize that until they're already behind. If your student is starting to struggle, a Pre-Calculus tutor in Northridge can help before things snowball.

What We Cover in Pre-Calculus Sessions

Pre-Calculus covers a lot of ground in a short time. Our tutors at Launch Valley Tutoring work through all of it:

  • Trigonometric functions and identities, including the unit circle
  • Conic sections — parabolas, ellipses, and hyperbolas
  • Logarithms and exponential equations
  • Function analysis: domain, range, and transformations
  • Sequences and series
  • Polar coordinates and parametric equations
  • An introduction to limits as a bridge into Calculus

Sessions are built around what the student's class is doing right now. We don't jump ahead or review topics the student already knows. We look at their notes, their homework, and their tests to find exactly where things are breaking down — and we fix that.

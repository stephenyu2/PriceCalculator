Sessions at Launch Valley Tutoring are built around the actual AP curriculum. Topics include:

AP Calculus AB vs. BC: Which Course Is Right?

AB and BC cover overlapping material, but BC moves faster and adds several additional units. Here's a side-by-side comparison to help students and families decide.

Feature AP Calculus AB AP Calculus BC
Topics included Limits, derivatives, integrals, FTC All of AB plus series, parametric, polar, and more integration techniques
BC-only content None Taylor & Maclaurin series, L'Hôpital's rule, integration by parts, logistic growth
Exam format 45 MC + 6 FRQ (3 hrs 15 min) 45 MC + 6 FRQ (3 hrs 15 min)
Score distribution Roughly 20–25% earn a 5 Roughly 38–42% earn a 5 (self-selected pool)
AB subscore Not applicable BC exam also reports an AB subscore
Who it's for Students building a solid calculus foundation; most common first calculus course Students aiming for STEM majors or who want to place out of two semesters of college calculus

What Pre-Algebra Tutoring Covers

Pre-Algebra pulls together a lot of ideas at once. Our tutors work through the topics that give students the most trouble:

  • Fractions, decimals, and ratios
  • Proportions and percentages
  • Order of operations
  • Introducing variables and simple equations
  • Basic geometry concepts like area and perimeter

We don't just drill formulas. We help students understand what the numbers actually mean, so the concepts stick when test time comes.

Launch Valley Tutoring works with students across the San Fernando Valley. Sessions are one-on-one, paced to your student, and focused on whatever they're working on right now — whether that's a specific unit in class or a bigger gap that's been building up.

Pre-Algebra Topics at a Glance

Topic Key Skills Why It Matters for Algebra
Integers Adding, subtracting, multiplying, and dividing positive and negative numbers Signed number rules carry directly into solving algebraic equations
Fractions Simplifying, comparing, and operating on fractions; converting between fractions and decimals Rational expressions and fraction-based equations appear throughout Algebra 1 and beyond
Ratios & Proportions Setting up and solving proportions; unit rates; percent problems Builds the foundation for slope, similar figures, and linear relationships
Expressions Writing, reading, and simplifying algebraic expressions; combining like terms Fluency with expressions is essential before students can solve equations or graph functions
Equations One-step and two-step equations; inverse operations; checking solutions Solving equations is the central skill of Algebra 1 — strong early practice pays off immediately
Geometry Basics Area, perimeter, volume; angles; coordinate plane; the Pythagorean theorem introduction Geometric formulas require algebraic manipulation; coordinate geometry links directly to graphing lines

Who Pre-Algebra Tutoring Is For

Even students who are doing fine but want to strengthen their foundation can benefit — small improvements in Pre-Algebra pay off for years.
